## What I'm building

<a href="https://forksai.app"><img src="assets/forksai.svg" width="100%" alt="FORKSAI: upload a PDF, slides or notes, get flashcards with spaced repetition, and an AI tutor that teaches the chapter aloud." /></a>

Upload your notes, a PDF or slides, and [FORKSAI](https://forksai.app) turns them into flashcards, notes and mock exams, then schedules your reviews with spaced repetition (FSRS-6). Miso, the AI tutor, teaches a chapter on a whiteboard in his own voice, drawing as he talks and asking questions along the way. There are also past papers you can sit in the real exam's format, a planner that spreads your syllabus over the days before your exam, and an Android app in beta on Google Play that works offline.

How it's built:

- A React + Vite web app, a Next.js marketing site, and a Capacitor Android app built from the same code
- An API on Cloudflare Workers, with Firebase Auth and Firestore
- Gemini for generation, with Vertex AI as a fallback, and ElevenLabs for the tutor's voice
- Dodo Payments for subscriptions, Resend for email, and web push for study reminders

**Eon Fitness**: a fitness app themed on Solo Leveling, built in Flutter for Android, with light and dark themes. Everything stays on the device.
